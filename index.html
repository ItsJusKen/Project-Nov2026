Who are we?

Built for movement across every terrain.

EcoFleet Global is a pioneering manufacturing and supply enterprise specializing in multi-terrain equipment. Founded with a vision to bridge geographical barriers, we engineer robust machinery that powers logistics, exploration, and commercial operations across land, air, and water.

Established in 2015, EcoFleet Global started as a regional provider of heavy land vehicles and has since expanded its footprint to aerospace and marine engineering, delivering top-tier equipment to clients worldwide.

Mission & Vision

To deliver cutting-edge, durable, and sustainable equipment for transportation. Leading logistical and operational machinery globally.
